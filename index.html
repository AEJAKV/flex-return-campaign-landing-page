Questions, answered

When does my 14-day period begin?+

It begins on the date the loan funds are deposited. Your exact deadline will be shown in your confirmation.

What must I pay to qualify for the waiver?+

You must complete the exact payoff quoted by our team before your deadline. Your exact payoff may include accrued interest from the time you received the deposited funds to the time you pay the balance off, the debit fees and express-deposit fee if you selected express deposit.

What happens if I keep the loan?+

Your signed repayment schedule continues. The surety fee and all other disclosed costs remain payable under your agreements.

Can I return only part of the funds?+

No.

Loan approval and funding are subject to final verification and signed agreements. The applicable credit grantor, GoLoans’ role, costs and repayment terms will be disclosed before signing. This page does not change the terms of an existing agreement.
